# Syntriq commercial site and editorial plan

## Product narrative

Syntriq connects business meaning, rules, processes and insights in one application platform, with AI that works from application context. Ontology is the semantic foundation; GraphQL is typed access. ChargeWeave remains the concrete domain reference while generic mechanisms evolve behind contracts.

The commercial narrative has no finished/unfinished badges. Explain architecture and illustrative experiences accurately. Do not imply measured gains, live AI access, deployed customers, broad standards conformance or production readiness without evidence.

## Site map

- Home: main proposition, retained brand artwork, platform overview and selected articles.
- Platform: models, constraints, decisions, processes, history and analytics.
- AI & Studio: catalogue, selected context, authenticated cloud reasoning, capability broker, candidate workspace, journal and evidence.
- Insights: ten visual articles, grouped by platform foundations, AI engineering and applications.
- Applications: ChargeWeave/domain-package boundary.
- Architecture: accessible responsibilities and links to engineering contracts.

5. Business decisions you can explain.
6. From business events to coordinated action.
7. Understand your business as it was known.
8. Analytics with business meaning attached.
9. From an engineering request to tested evidence.
10. One platform, different business applications.

Every article has a clear business problem, concrete example, PNG visual explanation, accessible HTML text, takeaway and related reading. Prefer commercial clarity with sufficient technical substance to make the explanation credible. Length follows the explanation; avoid padding to reach a word count.

## AI story

Use the approval-condition example across discovery, context selection, candidate proposal, broker-controlled execution, synthetic checks, investigation and review. Retain a replaceable reasoning adapter, browser-owned task coordination and authenticated relay. Distinguish context, journal and cache. Optional external providers extend capabilities through declared contracts. Explain model proposals separately from evaluated policy outcomes and permitted commands.

## Publication gates

Generate content deterministically; validate links/fragments, IDs, metadata, image dimensions and public packaging. Check JavaScript syntax and committed output drift. GitHub Actions runs the gates before GitHub Pages deployment. Review rendered diagrams and page layouts where browser infrastructure is available. Preserve source files for future edits; do not publish internal contracts or scripts into the Pages artifact.

## Technical continuation

Website publication does not implement the runtime. Use docs/ai-integration-contracts.md to guide a separately scoped source-verification and integration effort in ChargeWeave/Syntriq. Reuse the existing semantic and process direction; do not infer implementation from these product articles.
