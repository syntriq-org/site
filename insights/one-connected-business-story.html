SYNTRIQ INSIGHTS / THE PLATFORM

One connected business story

Connect the meaning of your data, the flow of your work and the evidence behind every decision.

A customer exists in a CRM. An agreement lives in another system. An approval waits in an inbox. A report explains the result, but loses the decisions that led to it. The business is connected. Its software needs to preserve those connections.

Start with what the business means.

Syntriq’s organising idea is a shared model: the entities, relationships and rules that give an application its meaning. A customer is connected to an agreement. An agreement has conditions. A change has an effective date and a reason. These relationships provide the context for the work around them.

This shared language connects the application’s views, tasks, processes and measures. Engineers can describe a business concept once and link the screens and actions that depend on it. Teams can understand why a field appears, which rule applies and what a change affects.

Keep meaning, action and evidence connected as the business changes.

Turn definitions into coordinated work.

A model becomes useful when people can act on it. Application definitions describe forms, available actions, workflow steps and analytical views. Reusable components interpret those definitions, while specialist components handle experiences such as a network map.

Consider an agreement correction. The application presents the relevant facts, checks the proposed change, routes the review and records the outcome. The updated record and the measures that use it belong to the same story. An operator should be able to follow that story without piecing it together across unrelated screens.

Understand a result in its context.

A dashboard number has a definition: what it counts, its unit, its population and its time window. Connecting these definitions to the model makes the measure explainable. Connecting them to history makes it possible to distinguish the information originally known from information corrected later.

That distinction matters whenever new evidence arrives after a decision. It supports clearer conversations about what changed and why two reports may legitimately show different views of the same period.

A shared foundation, a distinctive application.

Syntriq’s product architecture separates reusable machinery from business definitions. The foundation handles common concerns such as interpreting forms, coordinating tasks and presenting evidence. Each application supplies its vocabulary, policies, scenarios and specialist behaviour.

ChargeWeave makes this approach tangible in electric mobility. Charging agreements, settlement corrections and operational analytics offer concrete examples of how a domain application can use shared components. The same architectural boundary is relevant to other domains, with their own rules and independent validation.

THE BUSINESS VALUE

A clearer path from business intent to application behaviour—and a shared way to explain the consequences of change.
