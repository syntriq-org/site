Analytics with business meaning attached

Keep measure definitions, time perspectives and source context connected to the numbers people use.

A number needs a definition

An activation rate can look precise while hiding important choices. Which agreements belong in the denominator? Does activation mean approval or a completed operational transition? Which period applies? How are late corrections treated? Without these answers, two teams can use the same label for different measures.

Syntriq’s analytical direction connects measures to the application’s business model. A measure carries its definition and context so that a chart can explain what the value represents.

Connect the operational and analytical layers

Operational facts supply events, identities and quantities. Refinement validates and aligns them. Semantic definitions then specify the grain, numerator, denominator, aggregation behaviour and scope of a measure. The visual component presents a selected view of that definition.

Bronze, silver and gold remain useful ways to organise data processing. The semantic layer supplies business meaning across those stages; it does not remove the need for ingestion, transformation or storage infrastructure.

Preserve the context of aggregation

Some quantities can be summed across compatible records. Ratios, balances and snapshots need different treatment. A measure contract should declare supported aggregation and relevant units so a reusable component cannot casually combine incompatible values.

Relationships also matter. Grouping by a customer, operator or agreement depends on the business relationship and sometimes its effective period. Ontology identity gives the analytical definition a reference back to the model it summarises.

Make time and completeness visible

A January activation measure produced in February may include information unavailable at the original month-end cutoff. A view should preserve its period and knowledge perspective. Completeness indicators should explain known gaps rather than imply that every record has arrived.

An engineer investigating a difference needs the definition, filters, source versions and cutoff context. That evidence makes a discrepancy traceable and supports a meaningful comparison between two views.

Reuse views through explicit contracts

A reusable chart or pane can consume measure metadata, dimensions and supported operations. The application package supplies domain definitions; the UI component handles presentation consistently. ChargeWeave can define charging and settlement measures without making the generic chart aware of EV-specific concepts.

AI assistance can help locate a measure and inspect its dependencies, but proposed analytical changes still need validation. Scenarios should exercise changed inclusion rules, boundary periods and aggregation behaviour before a new definition is adopted.

THE BUSINESS VALUE

Give every analytical view a traceable definition, business scope and time perspective.
