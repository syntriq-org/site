SYNTRIQ / Architecture

Clear contracts between connected capabilities.

A practical guide to the responsibilities behind the product story.

Meaning and access

RDF/OWL identities and SHACL constraints form part of the semantic foundation through a declared supported subset. GraphQL exposes supported data and metadata; it does not replace the semantic model.

Evaluation and execution

A versioned decision evaluator returns outcomes, calculations, reasons and missing information. XFlow/XState coordinate processes and states. Commands enforce permitted changes and declared duplicate behaviour.

Time and analysis

Effective time and recorded knowledge establish the perspective of facts. Measures declare grain, units, aggregation behaviour, filters, completeness and knowledge cutoff so consumers can interpret results.

AI engineering boundary

Catalogue discovery, context selection, candidate isolation, task journaling and capability dispatch belong to the workbench architecture. An authenticated relay connects reasoning providers; tools return evidence tied to candidate and source versions.

Browser workbench and service boundaries

Local simulation and engineering tools can run beside the preview without requiring a complete business backend. The authenticated AI relay is a separate service boundary. Business persistence, shared execution and backend deployment require their own contracts.

Architecture documents

The repository contains the editorial plan, platform boundaries and AI integration contracts. These specify design responsibilities and acceptance criteria; they do not claim a live adapter or backend implementation.
